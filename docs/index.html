HOW IT WORKS

From activation to execution in three simple steps.

01

Activate Squad

Enter persistent mode with /squad. Your command center is now operational.

02

Issue Commands

Natural language instructions. Squad analyzes, routes to the optimal agent, and executes.

03

Mission Complete

Exit with /exit when operations conclude. Squad stands down until next deployment.
